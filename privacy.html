Privacy Policy

Last updated November 6, 2026

PantryPilot ("we", "us") is a recipe and meal-planning app. This policy explains what information we collect when you use PantryPilot on the web or on your phone, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Using PantryPilot without an account

You can browse recipes, tick your pantry, plan meals and keep a shopping list without signing up. That information is stored only in your browser on your device. We don't receive it.

Information we collect when you create an account

  • Account details: your email address, a password (stored only in scrambled, hashed form), and the display name you choose.
  • Your kitchen data: your pantry, saved recipes, shopping list and meal plan, so they sync across your devices.
  • Recipes you add: recipes you import or type in, including any photo you take of a recipe and the link you imported it from. These are private unless you choose to share a recipe with the community. Shared recipes are public, show your display name and avatar, and can be rated and commented on. You can make a shared recipe private again at any time.
  • Comments: anything you post under a recipe. Comments are public and show your display name.
  • Safety records: comments you report and people you block, so we can moderate and respect your choices.

How we use your information

  • To run your account and sync your data between devices.
  • To show your comments to other people and to moderate the community.
  • To turn links and photos into recipes when you use recipe import (PantryPilot Pro).
  • To show ads to people who aren't PantryPilot Pro members.
  • To send emails about your account, such as confirming your address or resetting your password.

We don't sell your personal information.

Advertising and cookies

If you aren't a Pro member, PantryPilot shows ads from Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technology to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can learn how Google uses this information at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and turn off personalized ads at adssettings.google.com.

If you're in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, we ask for your consent before ads use cookies, and you can change your choice at any time. PantryPilot itself uses your browser's local storage to keep you signed in and remember your settings. These aren't used for advertising.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your information while you have an account. You can delete your account at any time in the app: open your account (the person icon) and choose Delete my account. This permanently removes your account, your synced data, your recipes and your comments. Signing out removes your synced data from that device.

Children

PantryPilot isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, download or delete your personal information, or to object to how we use it. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, let you know in the app.
